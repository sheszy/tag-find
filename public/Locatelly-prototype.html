Simple steps, better chances

Locatelly Guides

A quick walkthrough for owners and finders.

For item owners

1

Register your item. Add a clear name and useful recovery instructions, then print or save its QR tag.

2

Mark it lost when needed. Add a safe contact method and optional reward so a finder knows how to help.

3

Follow up safely. Check finder notices, use chat to coordinate, and choose a public meeting place.

4

Retrace your route. Add places you visited and mark each one searching or checked as you go.

For finders

1

Scan the tag. Open the QR link to see the owner's recovery instructions. A connection is needed to send a notice.

2

Share useful details. Describe where the item is and optionally attach a photo or location checkpoint.

3

Arrange a safe handoff. Use the chat and meet in a public place. Never share sensitive personal details unnecessarily.

Prototype note: Cloud-backed QR reports, email accounts, and secure handoffs require Supabase. Nearby push also needs VAPID setup and user opt-in. Partner Desk data stays in this browser; SMS and Google OAuth are not connected.

About the project

Locatelly

Locatelly helps people reconnect with lost belongings through QR tags, finder notices, and practical recovery tools.

Keep essentials tagged

Create a tag for belongings and provide a clear, privacy-conscious way for a finder to get in touch.

Make recovery easier

Use status tracking, retrace stops, photos, and public meeting-point links to coordinate the return.

Privacy reminder: Avoid putting home addresses or other sensitive information on publicly scannable tags. Share only the contact and location details needed to return an item.
